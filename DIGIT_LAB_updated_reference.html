READ-ONLY GUIDE

General Matches concept

• Use the green arc to locate the most appearing digit.

• Consider the two digits with percentages closest to the most appearing digit, preferably the second-highest percentage, and look for an increasing percentage variance of +0.1%.

Entry: Wait for the moving-pointer indicator to point to the third most appearing digit whose percentage is constant and closest to the selected digit, then trade Matches.

New note — Predictions 6 & 7 (statistical context)

These notes describe observed digit-frequency changes only. A digit's percentage rising, falling, or appearing close to another digit does not establish what the next tick will be.

Prediction 6: Watch how digit frequencies fluctuate over the selected sample; stable or converging percentages are descriptive patterns, not forecasts.

Prediction 7: Compare digit frequencies and their recent changes as historical statistics. Green/red highlights and pointer positions do not predict the next result.

Reminder: Random digit outcomes may not follow recent patterns. This panel is informational and does not provide trade-entry instructions.
